# Brokers: who to ask when the Fares page shows nothing good

Airlines publish their own prices for each cabin, the **published fare**, which is what Google Flights and the Fares page show. A **fare broker** or **consolidator** is a travel agency that can sell the same seat for less. It uses discounted fares that airlines give only to agencies under contract, which the public cannot book directly (in Japan these show up as 格安航空券, often IT or PEX fares). Such an agency quotes a trip to order. Asking costs nothing, but the price is only known once they quote.

This list was researched on 14 Nov 2026 for business and first class round trips from Tokyo (Narita or Haneda) to the four cities in `routes.json`: Houston, Detroit, Tel Aviv and Barcelona. Nobody was contacted: no forms, quote requests or sign-ups. Every fact carries the page it came from and the date read (2026-11-14). "Not found" means no page read says it.

**What the research found:**
- **Best first ask:** a small Tokyo shop that sells discounted business tickets from Tokyo and quotes to order (Inter Resort, entry 1). It is the only agency with a current published fare that names one of the four cities: Detroit, on Air Canada.
- **No published prices for the other three cities:** no page showed a business or first fare from Tokyo to Houston, Tel Aviv or Barcelona. Those need a quote.
- **No suitable broker outside Japan was confirmed among those checked:** every US premium-cabin consolidator checked sells tickets departing the US (or Canada) only, and one (Skylux) could not be checked. The online agencies that do sell Tokyo departures publish no agency-only fares, so they are price checks, not brokers (see "Price checks, not brokers").

## Left out
- **Mileage brokers** (sellers of award tickets issued with other people's miles; airlines forbid this and can cancel the ticket):
  - None was found selling from Japan or to these routes. Searches included "マイル 買取 特典航空券 販売" and "マイルで発券".
  - Any offer that says "マイルで発券" (ticketed with miles), or that books on a stranger's frequent-flyer account, belongs here.
- **US premium-cabin consolidators** that sell US (or Canadian) departures only, so they cannot quote Tokyo round trips. Each page below shows US-only origins:
